Asked often

Asked often.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?+

Walk-ins are welcome. Dra. Angeli sees patients Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 5 PM. Calling ahead helps us keep your wait short.

What happens on my first visit?+

A dermatologist checks your concern, explains what it is in plain words, then suggests a plan. Treatment only starts when you are comfortable.

Which branch should I visit?+

Whichever is closer. Bantay sits in the ERP Building on J.P. Rizal Avenue, and Narvacan sits in the Balay Puraw Building on the Old National Highway.

Should I book a consultation or a treatment?+

If you are unsure what your skin needs, book a consultation first. Your dermatologist will assess and recommend. If you already know the treatment, message us and we will confirm it fits.

How do I book?+

Call Globe 0997-984-2888 or Smart 0968-882-2788, or message us on Instagram or Facebook during clinic hours, Monday to Saturday.
